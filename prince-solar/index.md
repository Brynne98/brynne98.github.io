# Prince Solar

Solar monitoring for homes with a SunSynk inverter. The Prince Solar web
app at
[brynne98.github.io/prince-solar-web-app](https://brynne98.github.io/prince-solar-web-app/)
reads your inverter's data from SunSynk, keeps the history, and shows
what your system is doing. It is free while in testing.
