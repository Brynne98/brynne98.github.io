# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 22 October 2026_

This privacy policy applies to the **PrinceMed Paeds** app. The app is run
by Brynne Prince, who is the responsible party and Information Officer
under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

The app collects no personal information. Nothing you type leaves your
phone.

## What the app keeps

- The values you enter stay on your phone and are used only to show the
  result.
- Your light or dark setting is saved on your phone.
- The app has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. It makes no
  network requests.

## Patient information

Do not enter names or other details that identify a patient. The app has no
field for them and does not need them.

## Apple

Apple handles downloads and may collect data under its own privacy policy.
We do not receive personal information from Apple.

## Your rights

We hold no personal information about you, so there is nothing to access,
correct or delete. Deleting the app removes everything it saved on your
phone. Under POPIA you may still contact us, or the Information Regulator of
South Africa, with any question.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
