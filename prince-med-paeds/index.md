# PrinceMed Paeds

Neonatal calculators for doctors, nurses and midwives: APGAR Score, Ballard
Score, Percentage Weight Loss, Corrected Age, and Pregnancy and Gestational
Age. Works offline. No account.

All calculations must be confirmed before use. The results are not a
substitute for clinical judgment.
