# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 9 September 2026_

This privacy policy applies to the **Health Switch RSA** iOS app (the
"App"), published on the App Store under the bundle identifier
`com.brynne98.health-switch-rsa`.

The App is a directory of South African hospital telephone numbers and
speed dials for clinicians. It has no sign-up and no sign-in. We collect
the minimum needed for the App to work, and this page says plainly what
the third-party services we use collect on our behalf.

## The directory itself

The directory ships inside the App and is read on your phone. Looking up
a hospital or a number sends nothing anywhere. The App checks for newer
directory entries when it has a connection, which sends a timestamp and
nothing about you.

The numbers come from hospital directories that clinicians built on
Accurx Switch, carried over with Accurx's permission before that service
closed to South Africa. They are hospital extensions and ward numbers,
not personal numbers. If you find a personal number in the directory,
report it in the App or email us and we will remove it.

## Data stored on your device

Your favourites, your recent hospitals, your appearance choice and which
numbers you have reported are stored on your phone using the operating
system's standard storage. They never leave it.

## Contributions you make

Anyone can add, correct or report a number. When you do, the App quietly
creates an anonymous identity for your installation so that your changes
can be attributed to one another and, if needed, undone together. This
identity is a random token. It is not your name, email address, phone
number or professional registration, none of which the App ever asks
for.

What is stored on our server when you contribute:

- The number or hospital you added or changed, and the previous value.
- The anonymous identity of the installation that made the change.
- The time of the change.
- Reports that a number does not work, counted per installation.
- Feedback you type on the Feedback screen. Please do not put personal
  details in it.

Contributions are shared with every other user of the App, because that
is the point of a shared directory. Previous values are kept so a bad
edit can be reverted. Nothing you contribute can be traced to you unless
you put identifying details into the text yourself.

Our server is hosted by Convex (convex.dev) in the United States.
Convex's privacy policy: https://www.convex.dev/privacy

## Data collected by third-party services

### PostHog (product analytics)

The App sends a small set of events to PostHog's EU-hosted service so we
can see which parts of the App are used and where the directory has
gaps. These events include:

- App opens, App version and device model.
- That a hospital was opened, a number was dialled, copied or shared,
  an edit or report was made, or the paywall was shown.
- That a search found nothing, and how many characters were typed.

We never send the words you searched for, because staff names are in
this data. We never send your name, email address or advertising
identifier. PostHog assigns a random identifier to an installation so we
can measure return usage. Session replay and automatic touch or text
capture are disabled. Location lookup is disabled.

PostHog's privacy policy: https://posthog.com/privacy

### Google AdMob (advertising)

Phones without Pro see one small sponsored bar. It is served by Google
AdMob as **non-personalised ads only**. The App never asks for
permission to track you and never uses your device's advertising
identifier. Google may still receive:

- Coarse device and connection information needed to serve an ad.
- Which ad was shown and whether it was tapped.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from apps that use its services:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

### RevenueCat (in-app purchase processing)

When you buy Pro, or tap **Restore purchases**, the App uses RevenueCat
to validate the purchase with Apple and to remember it across reinstalls.
RevenueCat may collect:

- An anonymous user identifier generated automatically on first launch.
- Purchase history for the App: which product, when, and the price.
- Device platform and OS version.

RevenueCat does not receive your name, email address or anything you
typed into the App.

RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

### Apple StoreKit

Apple processes the payment through your Apple ID. We never see your
payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to that flow:
https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Children

The App is made for clinicians and is not directed at children under 13.
We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

## Your rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and similar laws
elsewhere you may ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected
or deleted, and object to its processing. Because nothing we hold is
linked to your name or email address, we may not be able to find a
particular installation from your contact details alone. If you tell us
the approximate time and content of a contribution, we can find and
remove it.

You can stop advertising entirely by buying Pro. You can delete
everything on your phone by deleting the App.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes or as the law requires. The
date at the top always shows the latest version. Material changes will
be announced in the App's release notes.
