# Health Switch RSA

The directory South African clinicians reach for first: every hospital
switchboard, ward and speed dial, on your phone, working without signal.

Free to read, free to contribute. Health Switch Pro removes the sponsored
bar and adds dark mode and favourites.

## Support

reported in the App itself: open the number and tap **This number
doesn't work**.
