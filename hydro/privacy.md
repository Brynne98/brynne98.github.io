# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 28 September 2026_

This privacy policy applies to **Hydro**: the web app at
app.rmwsystems.co.za and the Hydro monitoring devices that report to it
(together, the "Service"). The Service is run by Brynne Prince, who is
the responsible party for your personal information under the Protection
of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

We collect what the Service needs to show you your water and warn you
when something is wrong, and nothing more. We do not sell your data, and
the web app has no advertising and no third-party analytics.

## Your account

When you create an account, or someone invites you to one, we store:

- Your name, email address and username.
- Your password, stored only as a secure hash. We cannot read it.
- Whether the account is Personal or Business, and the name of your home
  or organisation.
- Your role in an organisation (for example admin, manager or user).
- A phone number, only if you add one to receive alerts by SMS or
  WhatsApp.

### Signing in with Google

If you choose **Continue with Google**, Google tells us your name, email
address, whether Google has verified that email, and your profile
picture link. We use them to create or find your Hydro account. We do
not receive your Google password and we get no access to your Gmail,
contacts, files or anything else in your Google account. If a Hydro
account already uses the same verified email address, your Google
sign-in is linked to that account.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Your devices and sites

- **Device readings.** Hydro devices send water readings (such as tank
  level, supply and pressure status), device health and connection times.
  We keep 30 days of readings so you can see trends and so alerts work;
  older readings are deleted automatically every day.
- **Sites.** A site is a place where devices are installed. We store its
  name and address, and a map position if you set one. When you type an
  address, the text you type is sent to Google Places to suggest matches.
- **Alerts.** We store the alert rules you set and a history of the
  alerts that fired and who was told.

Readings and sites belong to your home or organisation. People your
organisation adds can see them, according to the role they are given.

## Messages we send

- **Account email.** Invitations, password resets and sign-in links are
  sent through Twilio.
- **Alerts.** Alerts you ask for, by email, SMS or WhatsApp, are handed
  to our notification provider to deliver. SMS and WhatsApp are only used
  if you add a phone number and choose those channels. The provider
  receives the message and the address or number it goes to.

Twilio's privacy policy: https://www.twilio.com/legal/privacy

## Cookies and browser storage

The web app uses cookies needed for signing in and keeping you signed
in, and one cookie that remembers whether you chose light or dark mode.
We use no advertising or tracking cookies. The sign-in pages load fonts
and icons from Google Fonts and unpkg, which see your IP address as any
website you load them from does.

## Where your data is kept

The Service runs on servers in South Africa, hosted by xneelo. Database
backups are also kept offline by the operator. Google and Twilio may process the data
described above in other countries under their own safeguards.

## How long we keep it

- **Readings:** 30 days, then deleted automatically.
- **Your account:** for as long as it is open. If you have a Personal
  account you can delete it yourself in the app (**Manage account**).
  That deletes your login, your home, its sites and its device links
  straight away. Members of a Business organisation are removed by that
  organisation's admin.
- **Alert history:** a record of alerts sent is kept after the account
  closes. Email us to have yours deleted.
- **Backups:** deleted data can remain in database backups until those
  backups are replaced.

## Security

Traffic to the Service is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed, access
is limited by role, and devices must sign what they send. No system is
perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your personal
information we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA
requires.

## Children

The Service is for adults managing a home or a business. It is not
directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their
information.

## Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask what personal information we hold about you,
ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to its processing. You
can update your name and phone number in the app yourself (**Manage
account**), and delete a Personal account there too. For anything else,
email us. You may also complain to
the Information Regulator (South Africa): https://inforegulator.org.za

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service changes or as the law requires.
The date at the top always shows the latest version.
